# Demo scene assets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, October 1, 2026. The user supplied imagery was visual reference only, not copied into these assets. Original generated alpha was retained while encoding WebP at quality 92; UV cells separate the subjects at runtime.

- `public/assets/scene/fruit-leaf-atlas.webp`: 1536 × 1024, 3 columns × 2 rows. Top: orange, lime, berry cluster. Bottom: peach, mint, citrus leaf.
- `public/assets/scene/ice-droplet-atlas.webp`: 1774 × 887, 2 columns × 1 row. Retained for optional analytic presets without assigned artwork; unused by the default storefront.
- Default water and ice now use five optimized, user-supplied transparent WebPs. See [source mapping, alpha checks and export pipeline](clear-accent-assets.md).
- `public/assets/scene/water-splash-clear.webp` is a separate generated transparent water splash, placed behind all fruit, leaves, ice, droplets and the primary product. See [splash asset provenance and export](water-splash-asset.md).

Both are temporary presentation assets. Individual approved image/GLB replacements can be assigned through each scene node's `assetUrl` without changing animation code.

## Final fruit and leaf prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: single transparent photographic sprite atlas for separate floating fruit and leaves in a drink product WebGL scene. Create a perfectly regular 3 column by 2 row atlas, six equally sized cells, on genuine alpha transparent background, NO grid lines or labels. Each subject centered within its own cell with at least 15% empty transparent margin; no object crosses into adjacent cell. Top row left: one fresh orange wedge, juicy flesh facing camera at a slight 3/4 angle, thick realistic peel. Top row middle: one fresh lime wedge, translucent bright green flesh facing camera at slight 3/4 angle, deep green peel. Top row right: three compact photoreal berries, strawberry, blackberry and blueberry, grouped as one floating cluster. Bottom row left: a ripe peach wedge with peach orange skin and fresh yellow pink flesh, close crop subject but fully within cell. Bottom row middle: a single fresh mint leaf, gorgeous sharply detailed green veins and subtly serrated edge, slightly curved, pointing up right. Bottom row right: single lime citrus leaf, darker green glossy realistic smooth leaf, curved slightly, pointing up right. Style: high quality real macro product photography, crisp natural surface texture and moisture, physically plausible diffuse soft daylight, very subtle small reflective highlights, no cartoon, no illustration, no CGI toy look. All objects isolated floating, NO cast shadows onto any surface, NO text, NO brand, NO background color, NO frames. Need one consistent atlas with exactly six independent cells.

## Final ice and droplet prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: single transparent photographic sprite atlas, two columns one row, for floating ice and water in a drink product web scene. Left half: one real clear ice cube, 3/4 view, softly rounded irregular melting edges, tiny natural internal fractures and bubbles, transparent center with subtle cool pale blue refraction and crisp white highlight edges, no black surface, no milky or opaque block. Right half: one isolated small clear water droplet floating in midair, near spherical with slight vertical elongation, transparent clean center and a realistic shiny white crescent reflection on upperleft rim, subtle light gray blue refraction, no opaque pearl. Both have balanced macro photograph detail, soft daylight, commercial drink photography. Each centered completely within its own half with 18% transparent margin allaround. Genuine alpha transparency around and through the glass/water where applicable. No background, no colored glow, no backdrop, no gridlines, no labels, no text, no floor, no castshadow, no cartoon, no 3D toy, no otherobjects. Precisely TWO separate assets, clear ice onleft and clear waterdrop onright.
